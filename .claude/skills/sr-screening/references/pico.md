# Framing the question and the eligibility criteria

## PICO(S)
- **P**opulation — who, with what condition, in what setting. *Adults with STEMI and multivessel coronary disease.*
- **I**ntervention — *complete revascularization (culprit + non-culprit PCI).*
- **C**omparator — *culprit-lesion-only PCI.*
- **O**utcomes — name the outcome, how it is defined **and the minimum follow-up/timepoint**. *Cardiovascular death or new MI at ≥ 12 months.* Without a timepoint, a 4-month feasibility study and a 5-year outcome trial look equally eligible.
- **S**tudy design — *randomized controlled trials.*

## Writing criteria that can be tested
Each criterion must be answerable from a title/abstract or full text with yes / no / unclear.
- Bad: "good-quality studies". Good: "randomized controlled trials; exclude observational studies, reviews, case reports, editorials".
- Bad: "older patients". Good: "mean or median age ≥ 75 years, or subgroup data for ≥ 75 years reported".
- State language and date limits explicitly, and justify them (language limits can bias the review).
- Name each criterion with a short label (`population`, `intervention`, `comparator`, `outcome`, `design`); these labels become the exclusion reasons in PRISMA.

## Extraction fields
Decide now what you will extract, so screening and extraction answer the same question. Defaults: study_design, country, n_per_arm, population, intervention, comparator, outcomes, follow_up, effect_estimate_ci. Add fields only if you will use them in the analysis.

## Register the protocol
Register on PROSPERO (https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/) before screening. It protects you against accusations of changing criteria after seeing the results.
