# Getting the full texts

Try in this order:
1. **Open access:** the DOI link; Unpaywall browser extension (https://unpaywall.org); PubMed Central.
2. **Institutional access:** your hospital or university library, via its proxy or VPN.
3. **The authors:** email the corresponding author, or ask on ResearchGate. Most answer within days.
4. **Interlibrary loan** through your library.
5. Trial registries (ClinicalTrials.gov) and supplementary appendices often hold results missing from the paper; download supplements too.

Do not use pirate sites: legal and ethical risk, and reviewers notice.

If a full text cannot be obtained, it stays as "reports not retrieved" in PRISMA — that is acceptable and must be reported, not hidden.
